# Africa-first messaging proposal

## Positioning anchor

**Global opportunity, built from Africa. Starting in Nigeria.**

Kairos connects ambitious companies with skilled African professionals. We are starting in Nigeria, expanding across Africa, and building toward a future where talent can access meaningful work wherever they live.

This gives the brand one clear order: Nigeria is where the work begins, Africa is the current focus, and global access is the long-term vision.
